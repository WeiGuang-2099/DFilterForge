# Locked test result v1: the baselines

This is the baseline half of the locked test result: the five hosted baseline passes over the frozen test prompts, scored offline on 2026-10-02. They are the qwen/qwen3-32b A/A pair and the three slot winners the [test-run registry](../decisions/test-runs.md) names. **The repair half is not measured yet.** No repair request has been sent, every summary below records `not_measured.repair_at_1` as `not_run`, and repair@1 per model and arm will be added here when the [repair round](../decisions/repair-round.md) is scored.

Every number on this page was generated by a script from the committed files each table links, not typed: each pass's `scored/summary.json` and `scored/outcomes.jsonl`, its `run_manifest.json`, `completions/` and `attempts/`, the [registry](../decisions/evidence/test-runs.json) and the A/A [pair report](../decisions/evidence/aa-test-qwen3-32b-2026-09-26.json). The scored trees were written by `dfilterforge score` with the code at `0623248` in the no-network test image, and `score --check` reproduced each one byte for byte; CI re-checks all five and derives the pair report again from the committed passes. The [protocol](../protocol.md) defines every metric and rule used here and wins where this page differs. No dev bake-off number appears here: bake-off counts chose the models and are never reported as an effect ([bake-off note](../decisions/model-bakeoff.md), Not an effect).

## Ready gold

Each rate is the mean over the 40 ready cases of each case's share of items (its two paraphrases averaged); the interval is the 2.5 and 97.5 percent nearest-rank percentiles of 1,000 case-level bootstrap resamples, seed 17, on index vectors every ready-gold metric of a pass shares. The count after each interval is the matching items over the items it covers; every ready case has two items, so the count gives the same rate. A provider failure on ready gold counts against every rate below. Rates, differences and bounds on this page are rounded to three decimals from the six each summary records, a value exactly halfway rounded away from zero, so a rate always matches its count. Each run's `scored/summary.md` formats the binary float instead, which can show a halfway value one unit nearer zero.

## What this shows

- **Primary, C4-C2: the typed contract showed no consistent strong-exact gain over display filters when both had field context.** No model's interval excludes zero. The difference is -0.075 (qwen3-32b), -0.100 (qwen3.5-9b), -0.063 (qwen3.5-122b-a10b) and 0.088 (deepseek-v4-pro-0813). qwen3-32b's net count (-1 of 21 discordant) is within rerun noise, and its rerun does not reverse the sign. The nets of qwen3.5-9b (-6) and deepseek-v4-pro-0813 (+7) exceed the bound, but that bound is extrapolated from qwen3-32b, the two point in opposite directions, and 2 of qwen3.5-9b's 13 C2-better cases hold a C4 provider failure. So the primary comparison gives no model a typed-contract gain that both its interval and the A/A rule support; this negative result is published as measured. Sources: [qwen3-32b](test-qwen3-32b-2026-09-26/scored/summary.json), [qwen3.5-9b](test-qwen3.5-9b-2026-09-26/scored/summary.json), [qwen3.5-122b-a10b](test-qwen3.5-122b-a10b-2026-09-26/scored/summary.json), [deepseek-v4-pro-0813](test-deepseek-v4-pro-0813-2026-09-26/scored/summary.json); the [pair report](../decisions/evidence/aa-test-qwen3-32b-2026-09-26.json).
- **Secondary, C2-C1: no readable retrieval effect for display filters.** The three Qwen models give -0.063 to -0.013, every interval includes zero and every net count is within rerun noise (extrapolated for two of them). deepseek-v4-pro-0813's 0.138 [0.013, 0.263] is the only interval of the 12 comparisons that excludes zero, and it is confounded: its C1 lost 20 ready items in 15 cases to HTTP 429, and 10 of the 11 cases where C2 was better hold a C1 provider failure. It is not read as an effect of retrieval. Sources: [qwen3-32b](test-qwen3-32b-2026-09-26/scored/summary.json), [qwen3.5-9b](test-qwen3.5-9b-2026-09-26/scored/summary.json), [qwen3.5-122b-a10b](test-qwen3.5-122b-a10b-2026-09-26/scored/summary.json), [deepseek-v4-pro-0813](test-deepseek-v4-pro-0813-2026-09-26/scored/summary.json); [deepseek outcomes](test-deepseek-v4-pro-0813-2026-09-26/scored/outcomes.jsonl).
- **Secondary, C4-C3: field context did not raise strong exact for typed IR.** No interval excludes zero. qwen3-32b's drop, net -5 (-0.100 [-0.250, 0.063]), is beyond rerun noise, and pass B replicates it with the same sign (net -9, -0.138 [-0.275, 0.000]), also beyond the bound. It is the only reading beyond noise that has a rerun, since only qwen3-32b was run twice. Its interval still includes zero, so the size of the drop is not pinned down. qwen3.5-122b-a10b's net -9 (-0.125 [-0.263, 0.000]) is beyond the extrapolated bound, with an interval that ends at zero. qwen3.5-9b (0.038, net 0; 3 of its 8 C3-better cases hold a C4 provider failure) and deepseek-v4-pro-0813 (0.000, net -1) are within noise. Sources: [qwen3-32b](test-qwen3-32b-2026-09-26/scored/summary.json), [qwen3.5-9b](test-qwen3.5-9b-2026-09-26/scored/summary.json), [qwen3.5-122b-a10b](test-qwen3.5-122b-a10b-2026-09-26/scored/summary.json), [deepseek-v4-pro-0813](test-deepseek-v4-pro-0813-2026-09-26/scored/summary.json); the [pair report](../decisions/evidence/aa-test-qwen3-32b-2026-09-26.json).
- **Read the two retrieval comparisons against the field context.** The ranked list held every gold field for 0.350 of ready cases (mean recall 0.592, 16 names), and a non-empty list limits the prompt to its names. The two comparisons measure this retriever at this coverage, not field context in general. Sources: [qwen3-32b](test-qwen3-32b-2026-09-26/scored/summary.json), [qwen3.5-9b](test-qwen3.5-9b-2026-09-26/scored/summary.json), [qwen3.5-122b-a10b](test-qwen3.5-122b-a10b-2026-09-26/scored/summary.json), [deepseek-v4-pro-0813](test-deepseek-v4-pro-0813-2026-09-26/scored/summary.json).
