## Ready gold

Strong exact, compile valid, silent-wrong over all ready items and over-abstention are each the mean over the 40 ready cases of each case's share of items (its two paraphrases averaged). Silent-wrong over compile-valid items is a ratio of sums, as the protocol defines it: the silent-wrong case shares summed over the compile-valid case shares, which is the silent-wrong items over the compile-valid items. Each interval is the 2.5 and 97.5 percent nearest-rank percentiles of 1,000 case-level bootstrap resamples, seed 17, on index vectors every ready-gold metric of a pass shares; for the ratio, each resample sums both shares over its cases before dividing. The count after each interval is the matching items over the items it covers; every ready case has two items, so the count gives the same rate. A ready item whose request failed has no answer, and it enters each rate differently. It is a miss in compile valid and strong exact, so it lowers both. It stays in the denominator of silent-wrong over all ready items and of over-abstention without entering their numerators, so it lowers those too, where a lower rate reads as better. It is outside the denominator of silent-wrong over compile-valid items, which counts only answers that ran. Rates, differences and bounds on this page are rounded to three decimals from the six each summary records, a value exactly halfway rounded away from zero, so a rate always matches its count. Each run's `scored/summary.md` formats the binary float instead, which can show a halfway value one unit nearer zero.

## What this shows

- **Primary, C4-C2: the typed contract showed no consistent strong-exact gain over display filters when both had field context.** No model's interval excludes zero. The difference is -0.075 (qwen3-32b), -0.100 (qwen3.5-9b), -0.063 (qwen3.5-122b-a10b) and 0.088 (deepseek-v4-pro-0813). qwen3-32b's net count (-1 of 21 discordant) is within rerun noise, and its rerun does not reverse the sign. The nets of qwen3.5-9b (-6) and deepseek-v4-pro-0813 (+7) exceed the bound, but that bound is extrapolated from qwen3-32b, the two point in opposite directions, and qwen3.5-9b's reading is confounded by rate limiting: over the 37 ready cases its C4 failures do not touch, its net is -4 of 18, within the bound. ## Repair round

Each counted test pass's triggered items, the C4 answers to ready gold it got silent-wrong or invalid, were sent once more in each of three arms with the pass's model and settings, the frontier arms on another provider (Limits); the [protocol](../protocol.md#repair) and the [repair note](../decisions/repair-round.md) register the round. `resample` sends the C4 prompt again as a first turn. `bare` adds the counted answer as the assistant turn and the user turn "Your filter was incorrect. Reply with a corrected answer in the same JSON format." `counterexample` appends to that turn a card from the unscored feedback probe: at most three frames where the answer and the labels disagree, with 13 header fields each, or the error the answer raised. Arm answers are scored like any C4 answer and rounded as above. The baseline passes' `scored/summary.json` are unchanged and still record `not_measured.repair_at_1` as `not_run`: the round's numbers are in each pass's `repair/summary.json`, whose `summary.md` holds the per-item outcomes and transitions, and an arm run's own `scored/summary.json` is a scoring record, never a reported number.

### What was run

The 12 arm runs, one per model and arm, are complete. Triggered items, from each summary: qwen/qwen3-32b 28 in 20 cases (25 silent-wrong, 3 invalid), qwen/qwen3.5-9b 15 in 11 (11, 4), qwen/qwen3.5-122b-a10b 20 in 16 (14, 6) and deepseek/deepseek-v4-pro-0813 12 in 9 (4, 8). They charged at most 0.2139 USD against 2.70 USD of caps: the 12 bounds sum to 0.213833 USD, rounded up here, and the providers reported 0.2137 USD. ## Limits

- **The frontier arms were answered by another provider.** deepseek/deepseek-v4-pro-0813's triggered items and counted first turns are the answers DeepInfra served on 2026-10-01; all 36 of its arm answers were served by NextBit, where owner ruling OD5 moved its arms after the DeepInfra route answered the dev round's frontier arms with HTTP 429 only ([repair note](../decisions/repair-round.md), Owner decisions, 2026-10-04). Its bare and counterexample arms put a DeepInfra answer in the assistant turn and ask NextBit for the correction. Both endpoints are labelled fp8, but the serving stacks differ, and the pool mixes the two. NextBit was written as these runs' provider after the first test request, against the protocol's Models rule, though before any test repair request.
- **The frontier resample also measures a provider change,** so its 0 of 12 unchanged answers and its comparisons with resample do not compare with the other models'. Answer unchanged counts byte-identical text only: the same filter written differently counts as changed.
- **Card value reuse is a diagnostic, never an outcome.** 21 of the counterexample arm's 35 strong-exact answers hold a value their card showed for that field, such as a `tcp.len` or `ip.ttl` that no shortcut rule covers, and they count as repaired. A copied host address or ephemeral port scores as a shortcut; none did.
- **No A/A bound reads an arm comparison.** The pair's bound covers the condition comparisons only. The arm comparisons are read by the 10-discordant-case rule alone, and no arm run was repeated; the resample is a repair request, not an A/A rerun.
- **Per-model power is low.** A model's discordant cases cannot exceed its triggered cases: 20, 11, 16 and 9 for qwen/qwen3-32b, qwen/qwen3.5-9b, qwen/qwen3.5-122b-a10b and deepseek/deepseek-v4-pro-0813. The last could never reach 10, and qwen/qwen3.5-9b needed 10 of its 11.
- **The triggered set is the baseline's.** Only answers that ran can trigger: qwen/qwen3.5-9b's 3 ready C4 items lost to HTTP 429 scored `provider_failed` and are not in the round.
- **Provider drift.** The baselines were sent on 2026-10-01 and the arms on 2026-10-05. The served models and providers are recorded, not controlled.
- **No dev repair number appears here.** The dev round was a pipeline check on 12 ready cases and is never reported as an effect ([protocol](../protocol.md#repair)).
- **No CI step checks this page.** The baseline half's generator is uncommitted, and its guards refuse the repair plans and arm runs now committed, so it no longer runs on this tree; the repair half is typed. CI checks the files the rows link: the re-score step every `scored/` tree, `repair --check` each round summary and `repair-pool --check` the pool.
- **Claim boundary.** Nothing here speaks to other Wireshark versions, real traffic or protocols outside the recipe world: the results hold for Wireshark 4.6.8, the isolated profile and these synthetic IPv4 TCP/UDP/DNS captures ([protocol](../protocol.md), Claim boundary).
